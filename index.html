📜 Terms & Conditions

1. Platform Overview

Zulora Local is a 100% free software platform operated by Zulora AI connecting neighbourhood merchants, farmers, and customers for direct hyperlocal delivery.

2. Self-Delivery & Fulfilment Model

Sellers deliver orders directly using their own self-delivery personnel or local runners. Zulora Local does not employ riders or charge inflated buyer platform markups.

3. Minimum Order, Distance & Weight Pricing

• Minimum Order: ₹50 per store order.
• Distance Charge: ₹15 per km from store to customer location.
• Weight Surcharge: First 2 kg free of extra weight charge; for extra weight above 2 kg, ₹10 per km is charged for the extra weight tier.

4. Seller Compliance & Commission

Stores pay a 3% platform commission on delivered orders, settled bi-weekly. All listed products must be genuine and accurate. Counterfeit or hazardous items are strictly prohibited.

5. Dispute Resolution

Inquiries and dispute claims can be addressed directly to zulora.help@gmail.com or via phone/WhatsApp at +91 6395211325.
